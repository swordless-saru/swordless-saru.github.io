# Tri-Tail Digital

Independent Android apps built by Steven Anderson, plus the engineering
standards behind them. Every app here keeps your data on your own device -
no account, no server, no sign-in, ever.

This is a single, unified documentation site - use the sidebar to jump
between apps and standards. Every app's docs (privacy policy, data safety,
what's new, advanced guide) live in their own section here rather than on
a separate site per app.

## Apps

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Dragma](dragma/) | Spreadsheet-first budgeting. Your accounts and transactions live in an `.xlsx` file you control. |
| [Paraclete](paraclete/) | Nudge reminders that fire *around* a time, not at it - also spreadsheet-backed, same no-server model. |
