# Tri-Tail Digital

Independent Android apps built by Steven Anderson, plus the engineering
standards behind them. Every app here keeps your data on your own device -
no account, no server, no sign-in, ever.

## Apps

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Dragma](https://swordless-saru.github.io/Dragma/) | Spreadsheet-first budgeting. Your accounts and transactions live in an `.xlsx` file you control. |
| [Paraclete](https://swordless-saru.github.io/Paraclete/) | Nudge reminders that fire *around* a time, not at it - also spreadsheet-backed, same no-server model. |

## Engineering Standards

| Site | What it is |
